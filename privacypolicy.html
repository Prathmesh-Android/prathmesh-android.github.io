Privacy Policy

Pixeldrop Co ("Pixeldrop Co", "we", "us") publishes Voice Command Guide for Alexa (the "App"), an unofficial reference guide to Alexa voice commands with an optional voice-translation feature. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

The App does not require you to create an account, and does not ask for your name, email address, photos, contacts, or location.

Information We Collect

Information collected automatically

Like most ad-supported apps, the App and its advertising/analytics partners automatically collect some technical information whenever you use it:

  • Device information — device model, operating system version, language and country settings, and the App's version number.
  • Advertising/device identifiers — such as your Android Advertising ID, used by Google AdMob to show and measure ads (see Advertising below).
  • IP address and general/approximate location derived from it (we do not collect precise GPS location).
  • Usage and diagnostic data — which screens and features are used, app crashes, and similar performance data.

Microphone & voice data

The App's voice-translation feature is optional and only activates when you tap the microphone icon and grant microphone permission. When you use it:

  • Your speech is sent to your device's own default speech recognition service (on most Android devices, this is Google's Speech Services) to be converted to text. The App itself does not record, store, or upload your raw voice audio to Pixeldrop Co's own servers.
  • The App receives only the resulting text back from that service, which it uses to perform translation and text-to-speech playback within the App.
  • Recognized text and translation results are kept only in memory for that session (and in your saved Favourites, if you choose to save a command) — they are not transmitted to Pixeldrop Co.
  • You can deny or revoke the microphone permission at any time in your device's Settings → Apps → Voice Command Guide for Alexa → Permissions; the rest of the App continues to work normally without it.

Purchase information

Subscriptions are sold and billed entirely through Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment card, bank, or other financial account details — Google handles that. We do receive confirmation from Google Play of your subscription status (active, cancelled, expired) so the App can unlock premium features and remove ads accordingly.

Free (non-subscribed) use of the App is supported by ads served through Google AdMob. You can limit ad personalisation at the device level in Settings → Google → Ads, or via Google's Ad Settings.
