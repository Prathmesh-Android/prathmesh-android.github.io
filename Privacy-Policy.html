Privacy Policy for Voice Commands for Alexa

This Privacy Policy explains how the Voice Commands for Alexa application (“Application”, “App”) handles information. The App is developed and published by Pixeldrop Co (“Service Provider”) and is provided as a free application with optional premium purchases through Google Play Billing.

By using this App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. This App is intended for users aged 13 years and above and is not designed for children under 13.

Information We Collect

The Application does not collect or request personally identifiable information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Contacts
  • Exact location

The Application does not require account creation or user login.

Voice Recognition

The voice recognition feature is optional and is activated only when you tap the microphone button and grant microphone permission.

Voice input is processed by your device's default speech recognition service (such as Google Speech Services). The Application does not record, store, or upload your voice recordings to its own servers.

The App may rely on third-party services that automatically collect non-personal information, including:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App usage statistics (such as screens viewed and interactions)
  • Error logs and crash diagnostics

This information is used solely for improving app performance, stability, and overall user experience.

Ads and Third-Party Services

The Application displays advertisements using Google AdMob (Google Ads).

Google AdMob may collect non-personal information such as device information and ad interaction data in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy.

The Application does not directly store advertising identifiers on its own servers. Advertising identifiers may be used by Google AdMob to deliver and measure advertisements in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Users can opt out of personalized advertising through their device’s advertising settings.

Third-party services used by the Application include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google Speech Services (device speech recognition)

The App does not serve ads to children under the age of 13.

In-App Purchases

The Application offers optional premium purchases through Google Play Billing, including subscriptions and one-time purchases.

All payments are processed securely by Google Play. The Application does not collect, process, or store your payment card or banking information.

Permissions Used

The Application uses only the following Android permissions:

  • INTERNET – required to load ads and online content
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE – required to check network connectivity
  • RECORD_AUDIO – required only when you choose to use the optional voice recognition feature

The App does not request or use sensitive permissions such as Location, Camera, Contacts, SMS, or Storage access.

Data Retention

The Application does not store personal data on its own servers. Any non-personal data automatically collected by third-party services is retained according to their respective privacy policies.

Children’s Privacy

This App is intended for users aged 13 years and above. It is not directed to children under 13, and the Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child under 13 has used the App and provided personal information, they may contact the Service Provider for appropriate action.

Security

The Service Provider uses reasonable measures to protect information processed by the Application. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Users are encouraged to review this page periodically. Changes are effective immediately upon posting.
