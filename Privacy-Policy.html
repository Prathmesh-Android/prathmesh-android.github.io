Privacy Policy

App: Alex Voice Command & Setup (Android package com.voicetoolkit.alexaz), previously published as “Voice Commands for Alexa” and “Alexa Voice Commands Assistant”.
Last updated: 2026-10-26

In short: the app has no accounts, no sign-up, no purchases and no server of its own. Your favourites, history and settings stay on your phone. The only data that leaves your device is what Google’s advertising and translation components collect, described below.

2. The app is free, with no purchases

The app is free and supported by advertising. This version contains no in-app purchases and no subscriptions, and it does not use Google Play Billing. We do not collect or process any payment information.

3. Data that stays on your device

These items are saved only in the app’s private storage on your device. We do not receive them:

  • Commands you save as favourites, and the list of commands you played (Activity tab).
  • An optional display name you type in More → Your Name (used only for the greeting).
  • Your chosen theme and app language, your selected speaker model, guided-setup progress, and your Echo Test wake-word choice.
  • Simple counters that control how often ads and rating prompts appear, and a daily translation counter.

You can erase all of this at any time by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

4. Microphone, speech and the Echo Test

The microphone permission (RECORD_AUDIO) is used only when you ask for it, in two optional features:

  • Speaking a phrase to translate. When you tap the microphone button, your device’s speech-recognition service (for example Google’s) turns your speech into text. That service has its own privacy policy and may send audio to its provider to do the recognition. Our app receives only the recognised text. It does not record, receive or store your voice. You can type the phrase instead.
  • Echo Test. After you tap Start test, the app uses the microphone for about ten seconds to measure how loud the room is, so it can tell whether your speaker answered. The loudness number is calculated on your device and discarded. No audio is recorded, saved, transcribed or sent anywhere. The app explains the microphone request before Android asks for permission, and the test stops if you leave the screen.

Spoken playback of commands uses your device’s built-in text-to-speech engine.

5. Translation

Translating your phrase into English is done on your device with Google ML Kit. The first time you use a language, the app downloads a language pack (about 30 MB) from Google; the text you translate is not sent to us or to any server for translation. Google ML Kit may send limited technical information to Google for diagnostics and usage analytics: device information (such as model and OS version), app package name and version, per-installation identifiers, performance metrics, error codes, and which source and target languages were configured. Google states that it does not transfer this ML Kit data to third parties. See Google’s ML Kit data disclosure.

Your choices

  • Ad-free hour: you can watch an optional short video to turn ads off for one hour (More → Ad-free for 1 hour). Watching is always your choice.
